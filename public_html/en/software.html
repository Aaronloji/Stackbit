— Process

No surprises,
delivered in stages.

We don't disappear for six months and come back with something you didn't imagine. You see and use the software while it's being built.

01

Diagnosis

We listen to your goals, learn how your company works today and define the real scope of the project. Free of charge.

02

Proposal & design

You get in writing what's included, what isn't, and the price. You review the screens and request changes before any code is written.

03

Development

We build in stages with clean code, so you use and test the system or site while it's being built.

04

Launch & support

We launch, train your team and support the rollout. After that, maintenance and improvements.

— Ground rules

How we work.

The four things that usually go wrong in software projects — and how we make them clear from day one.

The code is yours

The system belongs to your company, documented and running on your own accounts. You're not tied to us to keep using it.

Fixed scope and price

Before coding we agree in writing on what's included and what isn't. If something new comes up, it's quoted separately — never discovered on the invoice.

Deliveries that work

You start using a first version with the essentials early on. No waiting months to find out whether it works.

You talk to the developer

No middlemen translating your request. Clear explanations in English or Spanish, without unnecessary jargon.
