— Process

From idea
to working software.

01

Diagnosis

We listen to your goals, learn how your company works today and define the real scope of the project. Free of charge.

02

Proposal & design

You get in writing what's included, what isn't, and the price. You review the screens and request changes before any code is written.

03

Development

We build in stages with clean code, so you use and test the system or site while it's being built.

04

Launch & support

We launch, train your team and support the rollout. After that, maintenance and improvements.

— FAQ

Questions before
getting started.

Stackbit is a remote software studio. We build custom software for companies — management systems, mobile and web apps, AI automation, dashboards and integrations — as well as websites and online stores. We work in English and Spanish with companies anywhere in the world.

Yes. From a business that's just getting started to a large company with several teams. A project can start small and well defined — one automated process, an inventory system, a dashboard with your key numbers — and grow in modules from there.

Off-the-shelf software makes you change the way you work to fit the program. Custom software does the opposite. You don't pay for modules you'll never use, there are no per-user licenses that go up every year, the features specific to your business can actually be built — and the code belongs to your company.

Yes, where there's a measurable result. Typical uses: answering and routing WhatsApp inquiries 24/7, reading invoices and documents to extract data without manual entry, generating reports automatically and answering questions about your company's internal information. We don't add AI where a simple automation does the job better.

Tell us about it. Platforms, marketplaces, digital products, prototypes to raise funding, unusual internal tools — if it's software, it can be evaluated. In the diagnosis we tell you honestly whether it's viable, what the smallest useful first version would be and what we see as risky.

We don't use price lists, because no two companies need exactly the same thing. Cost depends on scope, features and timeline. Tell us what you want to achieve and within 24 hours you'll get a tailored quote, free and with no commitment. Scope and price are agreed in writing before any code is written.

We accept PayPal, Stripe or bank transfer. Payment terms are set in writing in the proposal, before work starts.

Yes, the whole process is remote: video calls, shared documents and staged deliveries you can test. We communicate in English and Spanish, reply within 24 hours and are available during Americas and Europe business hours.

Before any code is written you see the visual proposal, with 2 rounds of revisions included. After delivery you get 1 round of adjustments at no cost during the first 30 days. You'll never see your product for the first time on launch day.

The timeline is defined at the start of each project based on scope and complexity, and it's written into the proposal. We work in stages, so you start using a first version early instead of waiting months.

We design and build from scratch. No generic WordPress or Wix templates — each project is built around your brand and your process.
